In Mission 1, my color rule put the box on the mug in normal_1 but failed in dim_1, where the desk matched the same value range and merged with the mug. Its box precision was only about 0.42, and the learned classifier also struggled, with precision 0.60 and recall 0.15. In Mission 2, a gain of 0.8 removed the overshoot I saw at 1.2, but the run took longer, 20.62 s instead of 18.99 s. In Mission 3, gain 0.08 completed the curved track, while gain 0.05 lost the line at 160 s because its turn commands were too weak. I infer that reliable behavior needs both good perception and well-tuned control, since a bad detection leads to bad commands and a wrong gain wastes a good detection. The main limits are that I tested only in simulation and on a small image set, with one run per setting, and that a centered color or a stable hold does not prove object identity or distance.
