# mission_2

## choice
I recommend the learned detector at a threshold of [your value] for further testing. It was perfect in normal and dim, while the classical detector made false boxes in both. It takes about 28 to 32 ms per image, compared with 2 to 4 ms for classical, but both are fast, and a quick wrong box is worse than a slightly slower right one. Its weakness is occlusion, so that needs more testing. For normal_1, the mask IoU is 0.58 and the Dice is 0.73. A mask doesn’t identify the mug because the model has no cup label. It marks anything that stands out, including the laptop and trackpad, and it even marks foreground in images with no mug. One limit is that the test is small, with only 31 images made from a few edited photos, so the results may not hold for other cups, desks or lighting.

## comparison
In normal, the classical detector got 1 correct box, 2 false boxes and 1 missed mug. The learned detector got 2 correct, 0 false and 0 missed. In dim, classical got 0 correct, 3 false and 2 missed, and learned got 2 correct, 0 false and 0 missed. In occluded, it flipped: classical got 2 correct, 0 false and 0 missed, and learned got 0 correct, 0 false and 2 missed. False boxes and missed mugs matter most, because a robot would go to the wrong place or never find the mug. In dim_1, the classical box covered almost the whole image, because the dim desk joined the mug in the mask. The learned detector still found the mug. In occluded_1, the learned detector made no box because the gray patch lowered its confidence, while the classical color rule still found the visible part of the mug.
